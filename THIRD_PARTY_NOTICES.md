The author's CC BY 4.0 scope concerns only eligible original compilation, processing, documentation, and other protectable contributions. No original source archive is included. Exact object provenance is in `project/provenance/source_object_manifest.csv`; `SOURCE_DATA_ACQUISITION.md` identifies omitted files.
