**Table 1. Study design and sample accounting**

| Design element | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Crop and geographic frame | Corn; 17 major U.S. corn states |
| Study period | 2000–2019; 20 crop years |
| Observation unit | State × crop year × NASS week-ending Sunday |
| Constructed full panel | 3,000 state-weeks |
| Active start-state frame | 1,782 state-weeks |
| Primary complete-case sample | 1,777 state-weeks |
| Active rows outside primary sample | 5 |
| Additional exclusions after sample definition | 0 |
| Prospective forecast state | Monday 00 UTC GEFSv12 0–168 h precipitation totals; five members |
| Primary outcome | Percentage points of total corn acreage planted per suitable field day |
| Primary exposure | One pooled IQR of five-member GEFS precipitation-total sample SD |

*Note:* Active weeks satisfy 5 ≤ previous-Sunday cumulative planted percentage < 95. The primary ratio outcome additionally requires positive DSFW and complete specified covariates. The five-row difference is the complete-case accounting; no later exclusion was added. Counts and definitions come from the study's sample-accounting and construction records.
